WITHDRAWN 2026-08-06. Do not read the numbers below as data.

Both columns are the same arm. `--settings` adds hooks and cannot remove them,
so the "unguarded" runs still executed the guard hooks from the user's
~/.claude/settings.json. That is why the two columns agree on every metric a
hook can reach: they were the same configuration, run 48 times.

Kept as a record of the mistake, and because a framework whose whole argument
is "publish the nulls" does not get to delete its own retraction. The fix, the
check that now proves the arms differ, and the full account are in
../README.md.
